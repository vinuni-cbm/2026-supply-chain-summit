About the summit

A half-day convening of supply chain practitioners, faculty and students on where the discipline is heading.

Supply Chain Shift is an industry–academic engagement workshop. Senior practitioners from Unilever, Google, Corning and VinFast join VinUniversity faculty and students to exchange ideas on end-to-end supply chain excellence, cross-industry leadership, AI transformation and future talent.

The morning follows the chain from planning to procurement, production and logistics, then brings these functions together in an executive discussion. Each session pairs short executive insight with a real business decision, followed by a networking lunch and industry showcase.

  • DateSaturday, 5 December 2026
  • Time8:30 AM – 1:30 PM (ICT, UTC+7), including networking lunch
  • VenueVinUniversity Campus, Gia Lam, Hanoi, Vietnam
  • ForSupply chain managers and directors, operations leaders, academics and VinUniversity students
  • HostsSCV Global, SCV (Supply Chain Vibe) and VinUniversity College of Business and Management

Themes

Four conversations

01

World-class E2E supply chain excellence

What end-to-end excellence looks like in practice, from planning through to delivery.

02

Cross-industry leadership

Lessons that travel between consumer goods, technology, manufacturing and automotive.

03

AI transformation

How AI and data are changing decisions, operations and the supply chain organisation.

04

Future talent

The skills the next generation of supply chain professionals will need, and how industry and universities can build them together.

Across the end-to-end chain

  1. 1Planning
  2. 2Procurement
  3. 3Production
  4. 4Logistics

Central question: How can organisations make better end-to-end supply-chain decisions in an era of volatility, AI and rapid change?
